# Fingerboard Media — X Post Bank

Campaign concept: sell sponsorship placements on a fingerboard that gets brought to
events, pitched in dead-serious out-of-home media language. The joke is never
acknowledged. The rate card is real, the spec sheet is real, the CPM is
indefensible.

**Tone rules (important — the bit dies without these):**
- Never wink. No "lol", no "this is so dumb", no self-aware caption. Play it like
  a legitimate media buy.
- Be specific. Millimeters, impression counts, tier names, refund policies. The
  precision *is* the punchline.
- Real numbers beat placeholders. Swap the prices below for whatever you actually
  want to charge, but keep them oddly exact.
- Use the 3D model to render logos onto the deck before people buy. "Free mockup,
  no commitment" is both the funniest and the most effective CTA you have.

Placeholders to fill: `[EVENT]`, `[LINK]`, `[YOUR @]`, `[DATE]`.

## Posting notes

- **Media is doing most of the work.** Every post that can carry a render
  should. Blank deck for the launch, numbered exploded view for the inventory
  post, and a fake-but-clean "partner mockup" for anything sales-related.
- **Free mockup is the conversion engine.** "Send a logo, get a render" costs
  you minutes and turns lurkers into people who now have a picture of their
  logo on your board sitting in their camera roll. Some of them will buy it to
  make the bit complete.
- **Cadence:** launch thread, then one scarcity post every 1–2 days until the
  event. Don't post the whole bank in a week — the drip is what makes it read
  as a real business.
- **The recap is the most shareable post.** Hold Phase 5 until you actually
  have event photos. "Impressions: 14 (+27% vs. forecast)" is the line people
  screenshot.
- **Do not put hashtags on these.** Hashtags read as promotional; this needs to
  read as operational.
- **If it takes off,** the natural escalation is a second board ("we are
  expanding inventory") and a fake quarterly earnings post. Don't reach for
  those early.
